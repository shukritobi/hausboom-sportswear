01 / The idea

Not just gymwear. A home for every game.

A modern Malaysian sports label can win by serving the space between global performance giants and cheap generic jerseys: credible performance, strong design, local community.

Start tight with versatile technical tops and bottoms, then grow through club/team customisation, tournament drops and athlete-led storytelling. The brand should feel equally at home on a pickleball court, futsal pitch, run club or coffee stop after training.

03 / Brand system

Built to scale.

The strongest launch is not one viral jersey. It is a repeatable system that can turn product drops, athlete content, club orders and events into one recognisable brand.

01

Performance

Technical silhouettes, breathable construction, movement-first patterning and a visual language strong enough to stand without a giant logo.

02

Teamwear

Low-friction custom kits for clubs, schools, social teams and corporate squads. One ordering path, one size system, one design standard.

03

Community

Tournaments, run clubs, open play, creator seeding and selective sponsorship — the brand grows where people actually play.

04 / Clubs & teams

Your colours. Our system.

A dedicated teamwear flow converts the demand already coming from football, futsal, netball, pickleball, paintball and school teams into structured orders instead of scattered DMs.

05 / Partnerships

Earn the badge.

Sponsorship should be selective, measurable and easy to manage. A central partnership portal can collect every proposal in one format — sport, audience, calendar, deliverables and requested support — then surface only the best opportunities.

06 / Launch

Be first on court.

Early access turns announcement momentum into an owned audience before stock lands. Capture email/WhatsApp intent now, segment by sport, then launch Drop 001 to the highest-intent groups first.
