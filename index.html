Brand direction / Hausboom DNA

Same energy.
Sharper language.

SportsBoom should feel connected to Hausboom without looking like beverage packaging placed on a jersey.

The premium direction keeps the base almost monochrome, then uses Hausboom's familiar flavour colours as controlled identifiers: piping, trims, collection codes, team accents and campaign details. That makes the link recognisable while keeping the product credible as serious performance apparel.

Why this fits / Strategic direction

The opportunity is bigger than selling jerseys. Build the system that turns local teams into the brand's distribution, content and community network.
01

Founder-sport fit is authentic

Azzim is already active in pickleball, so the move into sportswear has a real founder connection instead of feeling like an unrelated category extension.

02

Demand appeared before launch

Schools and teams across multiple sports were already asking for sponsorship. That gives SportsBoom a community acquisition engine before the first product drop.

03

Hausboom provides recognisable equity

The flavour colours and Boom naming provide a familiar bridge, while the restrained design language keeps SportsBoom credible as a performance label.

04

The website becomes operational

Commerce, team orders, sponsorship intake and CRM-ready data can all live under one premium brand front door.
