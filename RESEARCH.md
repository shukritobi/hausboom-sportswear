# Sportswear Launch Research — Azzim Zahid

Research date: 27 August 2026

## What the public signals say

The supplied Threads screenshot from **@meripergibulat / Azzim Zahid** is unusually useful because it shows the brand is not just an idea. It is already in active launch preparation.

### Current launch signals from Threads

- Azzim says he announced a new **sportswear brand around 48 hours earlier**.
- He is already receiving sponsorship proposals from **schools, football teams, futsal teams, paintball teams, netball teams and others**.
- He is looking for a **KL-area sublimation factory** able to handle print, cut, heat and sewing, with an initial quantity around **50 pieces**.
- He is casting **male and female models**, explicitly welcoming different body types and new faces, with a shoot planned very soon.

This strongly suggests an early-stage but fast-moving launch with three likely requirements:

1. Direct-to-consumer performance apparel.
2. Team / club custom sportswear as a natural B2B revenue stream.
3. A structured sponsorship / partnership intake so proposals do not live in personal DMs.

## Why this founder is strategically different

Azzim is not entering sportswear as a first-time seller.

- He founded **Bulan Bintang**, which has grown into a major Malaysian fashion brand.
- StoreHub's 2025 case study says Bulan Bintang operated **15 boutiques** and around **30,000 active resellers and dropshippers** at the time, and had achieved No. 1 TikTok Shop recognition in Malaysia.  
  Source: https://www.storehub.com/my/blog/bulan-bintang-menjulang-baju-melayu-tradisi-ke-mata-dunia
- TikTok Shop's 2025 Raya awards named Bulan Bintang **Best Fashion Seller**. Azzim described TikTok Shop as a place where commerce meets culture, storytelling and real-time interaction.  
  Source: https://www.bernama.com/en/news.php/?id=2413326
- Majoriti reported that the business had scaled from hundreds of garments to very large inventory and highlighted the operational complexity of selling roughly 1 million units in a short seasonal window.  
  Source: https://majoriti.com.my/berita/2025/03/06/3-tahun-039berumah039-di-gudang-turun-naik-bas-ipoh-ke-kl-jerih-perih-sedekad-azzim-demi-bulan-bintang

The implication: this new sportswear label should not look like a small jersey-printing website. It needs to look capable of becoming a **national consumer brand from day one**.

## Market positioning

The Malaysian sportswear category remains heavily led by global names. Euromonitor's February 2026 summary says Nike and adidas each held 13% value share in Malaysia in 2025, with Skechers at 10%.  
Source: https://www.euromonitor.com/sportswear-in-malaysia/report

At the local end, ELGINI positions itself around functional, affordable performance apparel.  
Source: https://www.elgini.com/about-us

There is also a large low-cost custom jersey market. For example, Malaysian sublimation suppliers advertise club jerseys from roughly RM35–55 per piece with low minimums.  
Source: https://firstprint.my/jersey/pickleball

### Strategic white space

The strongest opportunity is therefore not “another custom jersey supplier” and not an imitation global sports brand.

The brand can sit in the middle:

**Malaysian performance credibility + fashion-level brand building + team customisation + community-led distribution.**

## Recommended commercial architecture

### 1. DTC performance line

Start with a small number of versatile SKUs rather than a huge catalogue:

- Men's performance tee / jersey
- Women's performance top
- Training shorts
- Court shorts / skort
- Performance polo
- Socks / cap / lightweight accessory

The website currently uses concept slots called COURT//01, PACE//01, KIT//01 and MOVE//01. These are placeholders only.

### 2. Teamwear

Give clubs and teams a dedicated path instead of making them DM:

- Sport
- Team / school / club name
- Quantity
- Required date
- Colours
- Logo upload
- Names / numbers
- Design approval
- Deposit / checkout
- Production tracking

The screenshot's mention of ~50-piece sublimation production makes this especially relevant.

### 3. Sponsorship portal

Azzim is already being flooded with proposals before the brand even launches. Build a structured sponsorship form with:

- Organisation / team
- Sport
- Location
- Social audience
- Event calendar
- Requested cash / product support
- What the brand receives in return
- Proposal upload
- Deadline

Later this can feed an internal scoring table instead of a DM inbox.

### 4. Early-access capture

The announcement has attention before products are public. The website should collect that demand immediately and segment it by sport.

Recommended launch list fields:

- Name
- Mobile / WhatsApp
- Email
- Primary sport
- State
- Men's / women's / teamwear interest

### 5. Commerce stack

For the first launch, the website can remain visually custom while checkout is connected to the preferred Malaysian payment / commerce backend. The front-end should not be visually constrained by the commerce provider.

Later phases can add:

- TikTok Shop linking / campaign landing pages
- Affiliate creator landing pages
- Team order dashboard
- Stock / size inventory
- Loyalty
- Event registration
- Drop countdowns
- WhatsApp post-purchase flow

## Website direction built in this repository

The current concept intentionally avoids inventing an official brand name. No reliable public source found during research confirmed the new sportswear label's final name.

Therefore the site uses a temporary **SPORT//01** identity and makes that explicit in the footer.

Visual direction:

- Black + acid-lime performance palette
- Oversized compressed typography
- Strong editorial sports photography
- Fashion-first layout rather than generic e-commerce template
- Multi-sport language
- Dedicated teamwear section
- Dedicated partnership / sponsorship portal
- Early-access capture
- Fully responsive one-page launch experience
